# Privacy policy

[English](PRIVACY.en.md) · [简体中文](PRIVACY.zh-CN.md)

0.0.0.6 · 2026-11-03

The extension stores account names, secrets, algorithms, digits, periods and ordering. OTP and QR processing happen on-device. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking or upload to developer servers.

Chrome sync may synchronize configuration through your browser account, depending on browser settings. Data is not guaranteed to remain on one device. Web previews use separate localStorage.

The storage permission saves accounts; contextMenus provides account selection; activeTab and scripting insert codes only when you invoke autofill. The extension does not continuously collect web content or read browsing history to generate codes.

Backups and QR codes contain unencrypted secrets. Imports are validated locally. Delete accounts in the extension and remove exported files yourself. Uninstalling may remove extension data; back up beforehand.

Upgrading storage may temporarily save an unencrypted recovery journal on the device. It is removed after successful verification; failures or inconsistent results retain it for recovery. The developer does not receive this copy.

For privacy questions, use the project Issues page. [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ydq/auto-mfa/issues).
